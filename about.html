What is WheelSpinn?

WheelSpinn is a free, highly interactive online random wheel spinner designed to take the stress out of decision-making[cite: 2]. Whether you are a teacher looking for a random name picker[cite: 2], a casual group trying to select a lunch spot[cite: 2], or a host managing a promotional prize giveaway[cite: 2], our platform brings physics-based spinning animations straight to your web browser[cite: 2].

We believe utility tools should be powerful yet incredibly straightforward[cite: 2]. That is why WheelSpinn requires completely zero registrations, zero email signups, and zero payments to unlock premium elements like per-slice custom image uploads or personalized styling color schemes[cite: 2].

Our Mission

Our goal is to build an unbiased, fast, and mathematically fair randomization system that runs smoothly across all screen layouts, from mobile phones to massive classroom screens and projectors[cite: 2].
