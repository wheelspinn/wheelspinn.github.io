Cookies Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them on WheelSpinn, and your choices regarding cookie management parameters.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text data segments stored inside your web browser system by websites you visit. They allow websites to recall specific data, preferences, or navigation histories to smooth out your subsequent interactions with the tool.

2. How We Use Storage and Cookies

WheelSpinn uses standard browser mechanisms primarily to optimize structural application functions and run ad-serving networks efficiently:

  • Essential Session Storage: We use internal local variable processing states to manage dynamic inputs, custom label texts, spin speed adjustments, and list entries while you remain on the web application page[cite: 1, 2].
  • Preferences Tracking: Temporary parameters store audio preferences (Sound Toggle state) so the tool behaves as you expect directly across consecutive spins[cite: 1, 2].
  • Third-Party Advertising & Analytics: To support this platform as a 100% free tool, our advertising providers and measurement partners place behavioral cookies to display relevant promotions and evaluate site performance metric details[cite: 2]. We do not interact with or manage third-party processing tags directly.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can choose to disable or selectively turn off our cookies or third-party cookies in your individual browser settings. However, please note that blocking essential script-based cookies may occasionally modify how interface assets interact, and custom text structures might reset back to defaults if page reloads are triggered[cite: 2].

To learn more about adjusting tracking tags across Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge, visit your browser’s official help pages.
