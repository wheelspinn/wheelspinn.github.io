🔒 Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At WheelSpinn, accessible from wheelspinn.github.io, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by WheelSpinn and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.

1. Log Files

WheelSpinn follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this as part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, WheelSpinn uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

3. Local Storage

To store your custom configurations, wheel entries, text labels, and color choices locally on your machine, WheelSpinn may utilize browser Local Storage or session variables. This structural configuration data remains static inside your device sandbox and is never transmitted or uploaded to a backend server infrastructure.

4. Third-Party Advertisers & Analytics

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on WheelSpinn, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that WheelSpinn has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

5. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity.

WheelSpinn does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

6. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions.
