❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about WheelSpin — the free spin the wheel tool.

Is WheelSpin completely free to use?
+

Yes! WheelSpin is 100% free with no sign-up, no email, no downloads, and no hidden charges. Use it unlimited times directly in your browser — forever free.

Can I add images to the wheel slices?
+

Absolutely! Click the 📷 camera icon next to any entry to upload a custom photo or icon. Supported formats include JPG, PNG, GIF, and WebP. Images display directly on the slice.

How many entries can I add to the wheel?
+

You can add up to 20 entries per wheel. Each entry can have its own text label and a unique image. For most decisions and games, this is more than enough!

Is the spin result truly random?
+

Yes. WheelSpin uses JavaScript's cryptographically seeded Math.random() to select a winner each spin — ensuring fair, unbiased, and unpredictable results every single time.

Does WheelSpin work on mobile and tablets?
+

Yes! WheelSpin is fully responsive and works great on all devices — smartphones, tablets, and desktops. The wheel and controls automatically adjust to fit any screen size.

Does WheelSpin save my wheel entries?
+

Entries are kept for your current browser session. Refreshing the page resets to the default. We recommend bookmarking the page before refreshing if you want to return to a setup quickly.

Can I change wheel colors?
+

Yes! Choose from 6 beautiful color palettes: Rainbow, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Candy, and Monochrome. Select a palette in the sidebar and the wheel updates instantly.

Can I remove an option after it's been picked?
+

Yes! After a winner is revealed, simply close the overlay and click the ✕ button next to that entry in the sidebar list. The wheel will update immediately without the removed option.

How do I make one option more likely to win?
+

Add the same entry multiple times in the list! Each occurrence becomes a separate slice on the wheel, giving that option a proportionally larger area and thus a higher probability of winning.

Can I turn off the sound effects?
+

Of course! In the sidebar, find the Sound section and click "Off" to disable all audio. You can also use the floating sound button in the bottom-right corner of the page.

📖 About Spin the Wheel

What Is a Spin the Wheel Tool?

A spin the wheel tool — also called a random wheel spinner, decision wheel, or wheel of fortune — is an interactive digital tool that uses a spinning wheel divided into labeled slices to randomly select one outcome from multiple options. It brings the excitement of a physical prize wheel into any browser, device, or screen.

WheelSpin is one of the most feature-rich free wheel spinners available online. Unlike basic alternatives, WheelSpin supports custom image uploads per slice, multiple color palettes, adjustable spin speed, sound effects, spin history tracking, and 8+ preset wheel types — all without requiring any login or payment.

Whether you need a random name picker for your classroom, a yes or no wheel for a quick decision, or a prize wheel for a giveaway event, WheelSpinn has a ready-to-use preset. Every wheel is fully customizable — change the entries, upload images, and adjust colors to fit your exact needs.

The wheel spin result is determined by a truly random algorithm, ensuring fair and unbiased selection every time. This makes WheelSpin ideal for teachers picking students, game show hosts selecting contestants, or anyone who needs a trustworthy random decision tool.
